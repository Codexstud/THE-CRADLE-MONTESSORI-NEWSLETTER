KEY EVENTS

KEY EVENTS OF THE 2025/2026 ACADEMIC SESSION

The 2025/2026 academic session was filled with interesting thematic activities, such as:

01

Made in Nigeria Clothing

Nigeria is home to beautiful and unique clothing made from rich local fabrics. Let’s discover the styles, colours, and traditions behind our proudly Nigerian outfits.

02

Columbidae (Pigeons and Doves)

Pigeons and doves are gentle birds found in many parts of the world. We explored their features, habitats, and why they are often symbols of peace.

03

Unicorns

Unicorns are legendary creatures that have captured people’s imaginations for centuries. We learnt about the myths, stories, and symbols surrounding these magical beings.

04

Introduction to Construction

Construction is the process of building homes, schools, roads, and other structures. Pupils discovered the people, tools, and materials that make building possible.

05

Introduction to Animals’ Feet

Animals have different types of feet designed for how they move and survive. Let’s find out how feet help animals walk, climb, swim, and hunt.

06

Introduction to Laundry

Laundry is an important daily activity that keeps our clothes clean and fresh. Our wonderful learners were introduced to the simple skills and good habits involved in caring for clothing.

07

Echoes of the Past (Grandparents)

Grandparents are special family members who share love, wisdom, and valuable life experiences. Let’s appreciate their stories and the important role they play in our families.

06 CHRISTMAS SPECIAL

CHRISTMAS AROUND THE WORLD

“SAME JESUS, DIFFERENT CULTURES”

Christmas is a special season of joy, love, giving, hope and celebration. This year, our children will take an exciting journey across the world through our “Christmas Around the World” activity.

They will explore how people in different countries and cultures celebrate Christmas while discovering the beautiful ways traditions, clothing, music, food, art, and celebrations differ from one place to another.

Through this experience, each class will represent a different country and become little cultural ambassadors. The children will explore traditional costumes, Christmas customs, songs, dances, crafts, decorations, food and other interesting features of their assigned country.
